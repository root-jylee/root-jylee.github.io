Research

Physical-Layer Authentication and Root of Trust

We study authentication mechanisms that use physical-layer observations such as channel responses, spatial signatures, hardware fingerprints, and sensing measurements as roots of trust. Applications include UAV, satellite, maritime, and cyber-physical communication systems.

Covert and Low-Probability-of-Detection Communication

We investigate communication mechanisms that limit an adversary's ability to detect the existence of transmission. Our work includes multi-user cooperation, channel uncertainty, spatial heterogeneity, and sensing-assisted covert communication.

Physical-Layer Secrecy and Key Generation

We explore information-theoretic security, secure beamforming, artificial noise, and physical-layer key generation based on channel randomness, reciprocity, and signal-domain characteristics.

Physical-Layer Privacy for Distributed AI

We study privacy-preserving wireless distributed learning, including over-the-air federated learning, differentially private aggregation, MIMO artificial noise, and defense against membership inference and gradient leakage attacks.

Secure ISAC and Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems

We investigate security, privacy, and trust in integrated sensing and communication systems, physical AI networks, and intelligent cyber-physical systems where communication, sensing, learning, and control are tightly coupled.
