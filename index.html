About

ROOT Lab studies physical-layer security, privacy, and trust by transforming physical signals into roots of trust for intelligent secure systems. Rather than being limited to a particular communication medium, our research focuses on the common physical-layer principles that govern communication systems.

Our central question is: what can an adversary infer from observable physical signals, and how can we limit, verify, or control that inference? We approach this question through signal processing, information theory, statistical inference, privacy theory, and intelligent system design.
