About

ROOT Lab investigates how physical signals, channels, and hardware characteristics can serve as roots of trust for intelligent secure systems. Our research focuses on physical-layer security, privacy, and trust for communication, sensing, and distributed intelligence systems.

We study security and privacy problems in which adversaries attempt to infer identity, transmission existence, message content, or data contribution from observable physical signals.

Research Interests

  • Physical-layer security and authentication
  • Covert and low-probability-of-detection communication
  • Physical-layer secrecy and key generation
  • Physical-layer privacy for distributed AI and federated learning
  • Secure ISAC and trustworthy cyber-physical communication systems
