Research overview

Building trust from physical signals.

ROOT Lab studies physical-layer security, privacy, and trust for communication systems. Rather than being limited to a particular communication medium, we focus on the common physical-layer principles that govern communication systems.

Our central question is: what can an adversary infer from observable physical signals, and how can we limit, verify, or control that inference?

Covert Communication

Physical-Layer Authentication
