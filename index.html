Research overview

Trust begins before the software layer.

We study the common physical principles behind communication systems: channel behavior, signal statistics, mobility, hardware characteristics, interference, and noise.

These physical observables become resources for limiting, verifying, or controlling adversarial inference across terrestrial, non-terrestrial, autonomous, and distributed-learning systems.
